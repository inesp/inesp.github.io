{%- comment -%} A "More in " link at the bottom of a glossary word card. Finds the post by its slug (the filename without the date), so rescheduling a post doesn't break it. Prints nothing until the post is published (future-dated posts aren't in site.posts), so unlike post_url it can point at posts that aren't out yet without breaking the build. Usage: {% include post_link.html slug="scaling-a-system-reads" %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- for p in site.posts -%} {%- if p.slug == include.slug -%} More in {{ p.title | remove_first: "Scaling a System, " }} {%- break -%} {%- endif -%} {%- endfor -%}
