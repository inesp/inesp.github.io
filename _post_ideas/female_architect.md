But, of course, if lots of you have though about becoming a CEO, why do only few of you actually doing it? Because a 2nd katalizator is needed: a very good reason to risk it all. A VERY good reason. Usually, the very good reason is: societal rejection.

Let's say I have 2 options: I can stay in my home town and get a job a developer at a local company, they will pay me 150% of the average local salary. Not bad, right. It is just enough money to have a good local living, I'm not going to be rich from it, and nothing in life is certain, but I am pretty sure I can work in my local town for the rest of my life and live comfortably. Most probably. Or... I could work for 5 years here and save all the money, not enjoy my youth at all, to be able to put most of my salary into savings. Then, after 5 years, I'll try to mve to Sillicon Valley, I need to find myself a SF company willing to hire me, so I can get a work visa and then I can use my savings to try to start my startup. This is A LOT of work. The payoff could be worth it, but the payoff isn't guaranteed. So,... what do you do: do you take option A or option B? And also.. all your friends are in this town, you like your life here, you parents are loving and supportive, you've just started dating the girl you were in love with for years,.. the house prices are reasonable too. .. Who would leave such a life behind? 
But what if it's not this peahy, what if your family is actually very religious, but you don't believe in god, or your family is very sexist, but you don't want to get married at 18 and have kids, or your family is really traditional, but you are gay and they will not accept you. Or what if this is a lonely town, with almost no kids your age or practically no jobs or your family just yells at each other all day. This will surely make you very interested in option B. 

Option B is why many women go into IT. They don't want the life they are being offered, they want better. They are smart enough to make it as a developer. They were though to complay, to always behave, so they are getting good grades. They want more from life, not a little bit more, but drastically more than what they are offered at home, in their default circumstances. 

People need a very good reason to go against the expectation of society. That is why people in movies, who do shocking things, are shown as shockingly despaired first. The bigger the action, the bigger the reason for it.

So, this is why women join IT: they are either very supported by their families, who help them get a footing easily, who help them thrive in this world. or they are very despread to prove themselves, to turn their luck around.
